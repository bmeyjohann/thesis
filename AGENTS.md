## Working constraints

- New human camera profiles keep only the scene goal in encoder images, without an appended compass/distance strip; preserve older checkpoint and curriculum preprocessing.

- A requested scene goal must be visible in the camera and policy encoder input; a HUD compass alone is not a scene marker. Keep goal geometry non-contact and excluded from terrain scans; version stabilization/goal annotations in checkpoints and curricula, show identical processed images to the human, preserve raw frames, and retain old trial inputs.

- Human observation-only camera views reuse cached encoder inputs and prepared policy context, including reset-aware frame history. Never add privileged geometry/material labels, a second camera render, or navigation height scans to PHP-only display; label normalized depth visualization and encoder-input versus feature-vector differences.

- Browser playback speed changes wall-clock pacing only; adaptive display skipping must not skip physics, policy sensor acquisition, decision transitions, or learner updates. Show achieved versus requested speed and record speed changes; never promise real time from a render cap alone.

- Keep training, evaluation, wrappers and launch configuration consistent. Checkpoints should retain settings needed to reproduce evaluation; explicit evaluation overrides should remain explicit.
- Keep reward and intervention semantics task-local and deterministic. Preserve executed-action, intervention-mask and replay/demo alignment when changing collection or learning.
- Compare methods under matching observations, action limits, compute and evaluation conditions. For material-avoidance claims, predeclare unfiltered and material-crossing goals, verify actual baseline exposure and report the strata separately. Distinguish simulation, diagnostic evidence and physical-hardware results.
- Run GPU experiments one at a time through a file-backed sequential runner and trusted repository launchers; do not depend on the removed experiment-queue MCP. Check for existing GPU owners, preserve completed artifacts, and only stop this task's processes unless broader control is explicitly authorized.
- Keep credentials, local machine configuration, datasets, checkpoints and runtime state out of Git. Existing `codex/` scripts are local helpers, not a mandatory reporting destination.
- Do not create per-change reports or append correction histories. Update existing documentation only when the change makes it inaccurate.
- Interpret speech-to-text `1B` or `1p` as `wandb` in experiment discussions.
- Tie the Windows gamepad publisher to its training launcher so an abrupt launcher exit releases port 8794; identify any port owner before stopping it. Partial Windows staging trees must resolve sender imports from a complete Windows checkout, retain child stdout/stderr, and pass a real JSON-packet startup check.
