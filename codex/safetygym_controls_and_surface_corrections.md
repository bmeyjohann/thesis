# SafetyGym Controls and Surface Corrections

- Turn mapping correction:
  - `A` / `Left` must turn left.
  - `D` / `Right` must turn right.
- Surface difficulty correction:
  - Default SafetyGym runs should use reduced-slip ("grippy") surface settings to avoid adding an unnecessary manual-control barrier during intervention evaluation.
  - Allow opting back to original physics with `--surface_mode default`.
